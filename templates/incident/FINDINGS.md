# Findings: {{NAME}}

One section per conclusion. A finding is only **Verified** when a human has looked at the evidence and accepted it.
Matching log lines, a script's output or several AI answers agreeing do not make something verified.

Keep in mind:
- A request that succeeded doesn't prove data was read.
- A row in the database doesn't prove the attacker saw it.
- Missing evidence doesn't prove something didn't happen (logs expire, sampling drops lines).

---

## F1: <one-line title>

- **What we found:** one sentence.
- **Proof:** evidence file, line numbers, event/request IDs.
- **What the evidence says:** short excerpt or fields (secrets and personal data redacted).
- **What it means:** plain English.
- **What it doesn't prove:** limits, other explanations.
- **Status:** Verified (by whom, when) / AI-analyzed / Assumption / Unknown / Disputed
- **Next check:** what to look at to confirm or rule it out.
